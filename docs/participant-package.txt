BACKGROUND INFORMATION
Case Background
The ATM Theft — September 14, 2025
On the evening of September 14, 2025, at approximately 7:45 p.m., an ATM located outside a convenience store in downtown Toronto was involved in a suspected theft. When the store manager arrived the following morning, the ATM had been left partially open and appeared to have been tampered with. A preliminary inspection by the ATM servicing company later determined that approximately $2,000 in cash was missing from the machine.
Police began investigating the incident and reviewed surveillance footage from nearby cameras. The footage showed an individual standing at the ATM that evening and manipulating and battering the machine before removing cash and leaving the area on foot.
During the investigation, officers identified Defendant A, Daniel Mercer, as a person of interest. Mercer lives approximately two blocks from the convenience store where the ATM is located and had previously worked at a business located one block from the store.
Police also interviewed an individual who had been walking past the convenience store around the time of the incident. The witness reported seeing a person standing at the ATM around 7:45 p.m. wearing a dark hoodie and light pants. Although the witness could not clearly see the person’s face, they described the individual as having a similar height and build to Mercer.
Investigators also obtained cell phone location data indicating that Mercer’s phone was in the general vicinity of the convenience store between 6:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. on the evening the theft occurred. Mercer told police that he had been walking in the area that evening.
At this stage of the investigation, police considered Mercer a person of interest in the ATM theft.
